## 1. Spec Overview

| Area | Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | Mediocre | Burst lives in the 1-min Summon Demonic Tyrant window; weak outside it |
| Utility | Excellent | Fear, Axe Toss, Mortal Coil + Howl of Terror or Shadowfury, Grimoire: Fel Ravager |
| Mobility | Mediocre | Demonic Circle + Soulburn + Impish Instincts; Frequent Traveler = 2 Gateway uses |
| Survivability | Great (except vs melee) | Soul Link + durable Felguard; Dark Pact, Healthstone |
| Difficulty | Easy | Instant Dreadstalkers, straightforward rotation |

**Arena role:** survive and control first, peel for your team, then burst during Tyrant.

## 2. Midnight & 12.1 Changes

### Midnight baseline
- **Hand of Gul'dan** cannot be cast below 3 Soul Shards.
- **Call Dreadstalkers** is instant (Demonic Calling).
- **Summon Vilefiend can no longer be cast** — it is bundled into Call Dreadstalkers.
- **Grimoire: Felguard** removed → replaced by **Grimoire: Fel Ravager** and **Grimoire: Imp Lord**.
- **Demonic Strength** removed.
- **Apex talent – Dominion of Argus:** during Tyrant, every 2nd Hand of Gul'dan summons an extra demon. Maximizing HoG casts inside the Tyrant window is the top priority.

### Patch 12.1 (Season 2)
- Shadow Bolt +45%, Demonbolt +55%, Summon Gloomhound +35%.
- Diabolist nerfs: Chaos Salvo, Felseeker, Wicked Cleave, Eye Explosion −20%; Flames of Xoroth 4% → 3%.
- Drain Life drain +25%; Zevrim's Resilience healing +25%.
- Curse of Exhaustion and Whiplash slow → **30%** in PvP (was 50%).
- Bonds of Fel damage +100%, radius 8 → 12 yd.
- Dampening now reduces the health Dark Pact sacrifices.

## 5. Rotation

### Before gates
- Cast **Create Soulwell**.

### Opener
1. Demonic Gateway across the map.
2. Demonic Circle in a safe spot behind a pillar.
3. Fear the enemy healer (or to peel).
4. Build shards; Call Dreadstalkers on CD.
5. Demonbolt on Demonic Core at ≤3 shards.
6. Hand of Gul'dan at 3+ shards.
7. CC the healer (Fear, Mortal Coil, Axe Toss, or a teammate).

### Sustained
1. Call Dreadstalkers on cooldown.
2. Shadow Bolt to build shards.
3. Demonbolt on Demonic Core at ≤3 shards — on a target **without Doom**.
4. Hand of Gul'dan at 3+ shards.

### Burst (enemy healer in CC, 4+ shards)
1. Call Dreadstalkers
2. Grimoire: Imp Lord
3. Summon Demonic Tyrant
4. Hand of Gul'dan (repeat as shards allow)
5. Demonbolt if low on shards and Demonic Core is up

Everything except Tyrant comes back quickly, so mini-bursts are frequent.

## 12. Battleground Blitz

- Same Soul Harvester build and PvP talents as arena.
- Lowest-damage Warlock spec; play for **control and base defense**, avoid big team fights.
- No dampening in Blitz — CC + burst wins.
- Shadowfury and Howl of Terror on cooldown; Fear healers often.
- Drop Soulwells; place Gateways across height changes (e.g. lower → upper flag room).
- Keep Demonic Circle far back to drag melee.
- Don't carry flags (poor mobility); peel for your carrier with Fear + Curse of Exhaustion.
- **Capture maps:** Axe Toss → Fear to cap faster than killing.
- **Temple of Kotmogu:** solid orb carrier (tanky, Circle out of danger).

## 13. Notes for This Folder's GSE Sequences

- `Demo_Diabolist_GSE.lua` / `.txt` are built for **Diabolist** (PvE-oriented). For PvP the guide uses **Soul Harvester**.
- Those files contain `/cast Summon Vilefiend`, which **no longer works in Midnight** (bundled into Call Dreadstalkers) — remove those lines.
- `Power Siphon` is not referenced anywhere in the current guide — verify it's still in your talent build.
- A PvP GSE should follow the burst order: Dreadstalkers → Grimoire: Imp Lord → Tyrant → Hand of Gul'dan → Demonbolt, keeping CC and defensives on separate binds.
