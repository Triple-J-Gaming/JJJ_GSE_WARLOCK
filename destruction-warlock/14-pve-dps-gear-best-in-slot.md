Unless you don't mind the recrafting cost or potentially crafting a slightly suboptimal item early on, it might be worth waiting for a few days until things have settled.

## Ascendant and Nebulous Voidcores (Bonus Rolls)

Nebulous Voidcore work as bonus loot and guarantee a drop from the loot table of a Boss or a Dungeon. While the drop in itself is random, each additional Voidcore spent will guarantee no duplicate until the whole list has been dropped.

### Recommended Nebulous Voidcore Usage for Destruction Warlock

Nebulous Voidcores are a valuable yet limited resource and whilst in general should be used to bridge gaps in a player current gearset, it is important to remember that how quickly an item can be guaranteed depends on how many other items have been already dropped from that source.

Not all drops are equally impactful, so listed below is a generic priority list on what to focus on:

Voidcore Targeting Priority
1. Mythic-track Jan'thrazet, the Soul Fang
2. Mythic-track Venomkeeper's Horrific Cowl
3. Mythic-track weapons (1H or 2H);
4. Mythic-track trinkets (especially Gebbo's Bottomless Bag);
5. Tier set pieces missing from 2 or 4-piece bonus;
6. High-impact slots from your BiS list.
## Best Crafted Items for Destruction Warlock

Crafting gear main limiting factor remains the weekly rollout of Sparks, necessary for high end items, as such it is important to understand how to approach the topic.

Destruction Warlock Crafted Gear Priority
1. The overall best combination is Arcanoweave Lining in the Wrists slot and in the off-hand frill, but that is in the context of fully geared characters, and thus should be considered a long term strategy, better suited for players who do not require the highest power gain immediately, but instead opt for the least amount of recrafts and resource investment.
2. If immediate power is instead the focus, then crafting a Aln'hara Cane with Hunter's Ritual Stone using 4 Sparks and Wrists with Arcanoweave Lining at a lower item level provide the highest dps jump.

The missives used for each craft are the following:

- Thalassian Missive of the Fireflash for Weapon and Wrists
- Thalassian Missive of the Feverflare for any other slot

### Adding Sockets to Gear

In Season 2 Miasmic Jewelbinder is available as a trading option at six tokens from Great Vault, which can be used to add a sockets to any Head, Waist, or Wrist item.

## Crest Usage

Mistcrests are a seasonal limited currency with a cap that increases each week and is used to upgrade items to their respective maximum level in each reward track.

Due to how each item slot is designed, there is a difference in power, tied to either specific effects or simply higher stats. As a result the following sequence highlights the most valuable slots where to invest Mistcrests:

Destruction Warlock Crest Upgrade Priority
Weapon ➜ Trinkets ➜ Helmet/Chest/Legs ➜ Neck ➜ Off-hand ➜ Shoulders/Gloves/Belt/Boots ➜ Rings ➜ Wrists/Back
