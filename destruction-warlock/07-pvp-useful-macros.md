# Destruction Warlock PvP Useful Macros

Source: https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/destruction-warlock-pvp-useful-macros
Retrieved: 2026-10-03

Macros are crucial to be successful in PvP. They allow players to use abilities on enemies without having to target them. This helps increase overall damage/healing and general awareness in the arena. Here are a few macros that all Destruction Warlocks can benefit from.

## Collection of PvP Macros for Destruction Warlock
### Fear 123 Macro
Fear Arena1 Macro
#showtooltip Fear
/cast [target=arena1] Fear
 Copy

Using this macro will cast Fear on arena enemy 1. This makes it easier to crowd control an enemy while staying focused on your target. If you want macros for the second and third enemies, simply change the arena1 to arena2 or arena3.

### Cursor Cataclysm Macro
Cataclysm Mouseover Macro
#showtooltip Cataclysm
/cast [@cursor] Cataclysm
 Copy

Using this macro will cast Cataclysm at your cursor without loading the green circle, making sure it casts faster than it otherwise would.

### Fear Focus Macro
Fear Focus Macro
#showtooltip Fear
/cast [target=focus] Fear
 Copy

If you find that you do not have much room on your action bars, you can use a focus macro. This is much simpler to use than the arena123 macro. Put an enemy on focus, and this macro will cast Fear on that player while you are still focused on your target.

### Spell Lock Focus Macro
Spell Lock Arena1 Macro
#showtooltip Spell Lock
/cast [target=arena1] Spell Lock
 Copy

This macro will use Spell Lock on whichever enemy you have on focus. Because you will be primarily using Spell Lock on the enemy healer, you should only need this focus macro.

### Havoc Focus Macro
Havoc Arena1 Macro
#showtooltip Havoc
/cast [target=arena1] Havoc
 Copy

This makes it so you can use Havoc on your focus target while still damaging your main target.

### Mouseover Banish Macro
Mouseover Banish Macro
#showtooltip Banish
/cast [@mouseover] Banish
 Copy

This makes it so you can use Banish on another Warlock's demon or an elemental pet while still damaging your main target.

### Cursor Shadowfury Macro
Shadowfury Mouseover Macro
#showtooltip Shadowfury
/cast [@cursor] Shadowfury
 Copy

This makes it so you cast Shadowfury at your cursor without loading the green targeting circle, making your globals smoother and more fluid.
