On this page, we list the best gems, enchants, flasks, potions, and food you can get for your Destruction Warlock, based on your stat priority. We also give budget alternatives. This page is updated for World of Warcraft Patch 12.1.

## Recommended Gems

Socketable gems are divided in two categories: Epic and Rare. The former offer a powerful effect but is limited to 1 equipped, whereas the latter includes single or two secondaries stats combination gems.

Gems can be applied to a Helm, Bracer, or Belt via the Miasmic Jewelbinder item from the Great Vault.

The Dazzling Diamond Epic gems of choice are Indecipherable Eversong Diamond or Powerful Eversong Diamond. The former is better until Mythic track gear, at which point the latter performs better.

In terms of itemization, opting for Critical Strike gems yields the best result, due how strong that secondary stat is for Warlocks in general. Flawless Deadly Amethyst is your best gem to pair with the Indecipherable Eversong Diamond, while it is very important to activate the full benefit of the Powerful Eversong Diamond by equipping 4 different-coloured rare gems.

The four rare gems you should be looking to equip are:

- Peridots: Flawless Deadly Peridot
- Lapis: Flawless Deadly Lapis
- Amethysts: Flawless Quick Amethyst
- Garnets: Flawless Quick Garnet

## Optimal Consumables

In addition to enchants and improvements to your gear, making sure you come prepared with the right consumables to endgame content has a big impact on your performance.

### Flask

For Season 2, Flask of the Shattered Sun is the default choice.

Flask of the Magisters gains a lot of value though when using the Freightrunner's Flask trinket and Enchant Weapon - Arcane Mastery, so make sure to adjust your consumables.

### Potions

Potion of Recklessness should be the first choice if your gearset has little to no Versatility, otherwise use Light's Potential.

Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion is the healing potion of choice.

### Food

Royal Roast and Harandar Celebration are the recommended food to consume. You can simply buy whichever is the cheapest, or eat from a feast when it is provided for the group.

Hearty version of food should be considered in situations where wiping/dying is rather frequent, as those persists through death.

### Augment Rune

Void-Touched Augment Rune provide around 1% dps increase and should be evaluated during progress, especially when close to pivotal kills.
