## The Basics of Destruction Warlock

This section is intended for players who are new to the game or class, have no intentions of raiding Mythic difficulty, or simply want a more straightforward way to play their specialization without being overwhelmed by the numerous priorities and active abilities that need to be taken into consideration for optimal play.

Destruction is a flexible specialization that can provide a variety of damage types and pressure without much rotational complexity. In this section, we will be focusing on a setup that minimizes the number of buttons and reactivity necessary without unduly compromising overall damage output.

Listed below are three simple rules to help you understand the spec:

- Maintain Immolate
Immolate is a deceptively powerful DoT that generate a good amount of Soul Shards during a fight. Make use of Cataclysm and Roaring Blaze to apply and extend Immolate on every target.
- Maximise Havoc and Mayhem windows
Since Havoc duplicates your single target spells, it is very achievable to react to a Mayhem proc and guarantee a double Conflagrate and a Chaos Bolt or two Incinerates for additional shard generation. Remember that Havoc also duplicates the Shard generation of spells.
- Be Flexible in your Approach
Destruction has a limited ability to pool resources and a Shard flow fast enough to allow to recharge them quickly afterwards. Use it to your advantage in order to burst down key targets when needed. Havoc and Mayhem allow you to single target without losing too much damage in AoE.

### Single-Target Rotation
1. Cast Summon Infernal on cooldown.
2. Maintain Immolate at all times.
3. Cast Chaos Bolt if you have 5 Soul Shards.
4. Cast Conflagrate if you have 2 charges.
5. Cast Cataclysm when available.
6. Cast Conflagrate to generate Soul Shards.
7. Cast Incinerate to generate Soul Shards.

### Multi-Target Rotation

Maintain Immolate on up to 3 targets by using Conflagrate on the main target with an Immolate active. Rain of Fire should become your main Soul Shard-consuming spell with at least 5 targets stacked together. Keep in mind there will be situations where using Chaos Bolt will be more beneficial, such as targets constantly moving away from the Rain of Fire area.

### Combat Assistant For Destruction Warlock

The Combat Assistant is comprised of two main features:

- Assisted Highlight: Displays the next spell to be queued, in a adequately accurate rotation single target and limited capabilities in multitarget, best used to familiarize with the flow and cadence of the spec.
- Single Button Assistant: Enables use of a single input for multiple skills in the sequence prompted by the Assisted Highlight, at the expense of a longer base global cooldown (which accounts for an additional 5% to 10% dps loss)

It is worth pointing out that these tools are accessibility features first and foremost and should not be considered a direct replacement to learn a spec.

The areas where the Combat Assistant struggle the most for Destruction Warlock are:

- DoT uptime, since there is no supported logic for the Pandemic mechanic, especially aggravating in multi-target
- Multi-target in general, due how it considers all targets stacked regardless of the situation
### Combat Assistant Tips
- Cooldown alignment has to be done manually, especially considering racials, potions, and on-use equipment
- DoTs should be refreshed within Pandemic range, as following the Combat Assistant blindly will result in much lower uptime
- Conflagrate strength comes from the mobility associated with its usage, best to keep 1 charge available when movement occurs
- In general, try to maximize the amount of time spent casting, especially when using the Single Button Assistant, as downtime is one of the improvement areas at any stage of the game
