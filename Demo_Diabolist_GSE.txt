NOTES
- Hand of Gul'dan becomes Ruination and Shadow Bolt becomes Infernal Bolt
  during Diabolist procs, so the same /cast lines fire them automatically.
- No modifiers: everything fires from the one key. Tyrant sits late in the
  loop so Dreadstalkers, Vilefiend and a few Hand of Gul'dan imp waves
  usually land first. It casts whenever the loop reaches it off cooldown.
- Felguard (Felstorm / Axe Toss) runs on pet autocast; keep Felstorm on auto.
- Defensives (Unending Resolve, Dark Pact, Mortal Coil, Shadowfury,
  Demonic Circle/Gateway) stay on their own keybinds.
- Remove the /use 13 / 14 lines if your trinkets are passive or you
  want to line them up with Tyrant manually.
