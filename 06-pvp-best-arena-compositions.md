# Destruction Warlock PvP Best Arena Compositions

Source: https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/destruction-warlock-pvp-best-arena-compositions
Retrieved: 2026-10-03

Team composition in 2v2 or 3v3 Arena is very important, because not all classes work well together. Here, we give you the best compositions you can have for your Destruction Warlock (i.e., the best classes to pair with it).

## 2v2 Arena

In 2v2 Arena, there are not many options. Your goal is to avoid as much damage as possible by kiting with Demonic Circle: Teleport and Curse of Exhaustion while throwing out instant damage with Conflagrate, Shadowburn and Wither. Creating windows to cast Chaos Bolt can help close games out. It is important to note that if you are interrupted while casting Fear, you can still cast Chaos Bolt.

Here is a list of the best team compositions for 2v2:

Warlock + Priest
Destruction Warlock Discipline Priest
Warlock + Evoker
Destruction Warlock Preservation Evoker
Warlock + Druid
Destruction Warlock Restoration Druid
## 3v3 Arena

Your role in 3v3 is similar to 2v2 Arena. You are trying to find a time to use Mortal Coil on both DPS and cast multiple Chaos Bolts on your kill target.

In the meantime, avoid damage by utilizing Fear and your mobility. It is best to be playing with another caster so you are not the only person being interrupted. Use Fear on the enemy healer and have one of your teammates stun your kill target.

It is important to note that Fear should be cast almost always unless someone is already Feared. This allows you to cast Chaos Bolt as the Feared person cannot interrupt, and allows you to bait interrupts from other players on the enemy team.

MLP
Frost Mage Destruction Warlock Discipline Priest
LSP
